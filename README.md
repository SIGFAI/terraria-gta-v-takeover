# GTA V Takeover

**Terraria** × GTA V · visual · idea by the team

Bring GTA V into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I turned Terraria into Los Santos: sports cars run over gangsters, cops swarm at 5 stars, and I shot down a police chopper with rockets.

## Install

Copy Sigf.tmod into Documents/My Games/Terraria/tModLoader/Mods, enable "SIGF Mix" in tModLoader's Manage Mods menu, then start any world. Los Santos appears around spawn.
